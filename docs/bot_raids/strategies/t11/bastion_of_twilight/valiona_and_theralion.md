# Valiona and Theralion — Phase 0 research contract v1

Scope: Cataclysm Classic 4.4.2-labelled behavior for Bastion of Twilight in 10-player Normal (10N), 10-player Heroic (10H), 25-player Normal (25N), and 25-player Heroic (25H). This is a researched planning contract, not a live-validation result. The official 4.4.2 notes do not freeze a client data build, encounter hotfix cutoff, or Valiona/Theralion tuning table; exact retail values therefore remain fail-closed where sources disagree.

## Contract

- Treat the pair as one shared-health encounter. Valiona begins grounded and Theralion begins airborne; one dragon is grounded while the other is airborne, and they exchange jobs through timed transitions. Keep the grounded dragon near the room center, and continue damage-over-time effects on both only when the target is valid and safe.
- During Valiona ground, spread around the boss for airborne Theralion's splash, collapse on a Blackout target before dispel/expiry, and move behind Valiona or farther away when Devouring Flames faces the raid. Do not leave the tank inside the Blackout soak unless the selected plan explicitly calls for it.
- During Theralion ground, keep ranged/healers at least 10 yards from one another, move Fabulous Flames out of the group, and move an Engulfing Magic target away from allies. Soak Twilight Meteorite with the assigned melee group; a targeted ranged player must reach that group before impact.
- During either transition, leave Dazzling Destruction and Twilight Flames/Deep Breath lanes. Players intentionally sent to the Twilight Realm must avoid Unstable Twilight orbs and use a Collapsing Twilight Portal; do not assume portal position, orb damage, or realm duration without the validated spell data.
- Heroic requires a second tank/taunt handoff for five Twilight Shift stacks. Send the current tank (and only the assigned realm team) into the Twilight Realm on Shifting Reality, keep the rest of the raid away from the five-stack trigger, and kill Twilight Sentries before their Rift Blast pressure accumulates. The local periodic cadence and all spell coefficients remain unresolved.

## Observable mechanics and targeting

### Shared health, phases, and enrage

- Repository spell IDs 90345 (`SPELL_SHARE_HEALTH_2`) and 90346 (`SPELL_SHARE_HEALTH_1`) are cast area-wide by Valiona and Theralion on reset and re-established when the sibling's share-health aura is received. The scripts use one instance boss state, `DATA_THERALION_AND_VALIONA`.
- Current Wowhead describes a 105-second Valiona phase followed by a 140-second Theralion phase. It places Theralion's three Dazzling Destruction casts at about 85 seconds in the first phase and Valiona's three Deep Breath passes near the end of the second phase. Historical Icy Veins reports 100 seconds and 140 seconds. Local events use movement and spell timers rather than those guide phase lengths; do not schedule an exact Classic phase timestamp.
- Both local AIs schedule `SPELL_BERSERK` (26662) at 10 minutes. Current Icy Veins also describes a ten-minute enrage. The exact retail enrage spell behavior and timing are not live-validated here.

### Valiona grounded / Theralion airborne

- Local Valiona schedules Blackout at 10.5s, Devouring Flames at 25s, and starts the 10-minute berserk at engage. Blackout repeats locally every 46s; Devouring Flames repeats every 41s. Current Wowhead reports Blackout at about 10s and every 45s, and Devouring Flames every 25s; historical Icy Veins says two or three Blackouts and two breaths in a phase, with a 40s Blackout cooldown. Treat guide cadence versus local cadence as unresolved.
- Current Wowhead describes Blackout as a 75,000 heal absorb, then 975,000–1,025,000 Shadow damage within 8 yards split among nearby players; its narrative gives 15s expiry and a 500k normal/1M heroic description, which conflicts with the ability table. The local `SPELL_BLACKOUT_DUMMY` (86673) selects one random non-filtered target; `SPELL_BLACKOUT_DAMAGE` (86825) is cast on aura removal by enemy spell or expiry and divides hit damage by area target count. Exact Classic absorb, expiry, radius, and mode damage are unresolved.
- Current Wowhead reports Devouring Flames (local targeting 86832, damage 86840) as 90,000 Shadow per second with distance reduction. Its narrative reports 64,000 normal/90,000 heroic. The local spell script reduces hit damage by a percentage derived from caster distance, selects one random target, and excludes Twilight-phase units; exact cone, tick, and mode coefficients are spell-data dependent.
- Local Theralion starts `SPELL_TWILIGHT_BLAST` (86369) 400ms after reaching the flight target, picks a random valid target within 100 yards, and repeats every 2.4s. Current Wowhead says roughly every 3s and 25k normal/35k heroic within 8 yards; its ability table reports 34,125–35,875. Historical Icy Veins says every 2s. The target/splash rule is qualitatively consistent, but timer and damage are unresolved.
- Local Theralion starts Dazzling Destruction after 1m22s in air. It fires three target passes: the first immediately, then every 4.7s, cancels Twilight Blast, and schedules landing 4s after the third. Local target scripts identify stalkers by visual aura (NPC 46374, visual 86383), cast the dummy (86408), exclude Twilight-phase units from the normal-realm hit, and apply a separate Twilight-realm spell (93063). Current Wowhead reports three casts at a 5s interval with a 4s telegraph/15k shadow example; historical Icy Veins reports two ground zones per cast and 4s casts. Exact stalker count/radius and landing time are unresolved.

### Valiona airborne / Theralion grounded

- On local Theralion landing, he re-engages after 2s, then starts Fabulous Flames at 3.6s and Engulfing Magic at 12s. Fabulous Flames (targeting 86495, local random target script) repeats every 15.7s; Engulfing Magic (targeting 86607, triggered 86631) repeats every 40s. Current Wowhead reports Fabulous Flames every roughly 15–17s, 12k Shadow per second and 35s persistence, while its ability table reports a 23,125–26,875 Shadow explosion in 10 yards. It reports Engulfing on 1–3 ranged players every 35s, +100% damage/healing, and allied splash within 10 yards. Historical Icy Veins reports every 15s and one target in 10-player/two in 25-player. Do not encode either guide's exact cadence as authoritative.
- The local Engulfing target filter removes Hunter, Warrior, and Death Knight classes and players carrying Twilight Meteorite targeting; it then chooses one target in non-25 maps or up to three in 25-player maps. This is a repository targeting rule, not proof that retail selection is class-filtered. The proc script converts the target's damage/heal amount into `SPELL_ENGULFING_MAGIC_TRIGGERED` (86631), so the local scaling relationship is observable while the spell magnitude and proc radius remain DBC-dependent.
- Local airborne Valiona starts Twilight Meteorite (targeting 88518) 2s after takeoff movement and repeats every 1.2s until Deep Breath cancels it. Its target script removes Theralion's current victim, Twilight-phase units, and then keeps one random target; the local hit script divides damage by the number of targets in the destination area. Current Wowhead reports 292,500–307,500 Shadow within 6 yards; its narrative reports 235k normal/300k heroic after a 6s warning. Historical Icy Veins reports a 5s warning and a 6s cadence. Local targeting explicitly excludes the ground tank, while current guide prose calls it random; target and timer are unresolved.
- Local Deep Breath starts at 1m25.1s after Valiona liftoff and runs a three-pass movement state. The first pass chooses the nearer Valiona/Theralion side, selects one of three repository lane waypoint pairs at random, announces after 200ms, flies to the lane after 1.7s, crosses after 3.8s with middle/top/bottom strafe triggers, and schedules the next pass after 1s. After the third pass, local movement prepares landing and schedules it 1s later. Current Wowhead places the transition after about 100s in air/40s before swap; historical Icy Veins places it at 95s/45s before swap. The local timings include movement duration and are not a retail 4.4.2 guarantee.
- Twilight Flames/Deep Breath local IDs are 86194 (trigger), 86144 (strafe), and 86077 (speed burst). Current Wowhead reports 15k Shadow and realm transfer on a failed lane; the ability table reports 29,250–30,750. Historical Icy Veins says the fire fills one third of the room and sends affected players to the Twilight Realm. Exact cast, lane, damage, and realm interaction remain unresolved.

### Twilight Realm and heroic additions

- Local Twilight phase ID 290 and phase group 525 are used to filter normal-realm and Twilight-realm targets. `SPELL_TWILIGHT_SHIFT` (93053) is applied to the heroic pair on engage; `SPELL_TWILIGHT_PROTECTION_BUFF` (86415), `SPELL_SHIFTING_REALITY` (93055), and `SPELL_TWILIGHT_SHIFT_ALLY` (93064) are applied by the local script when a unit reaches five stacks. Current Icy Veins says the tank is teleported at five stacks and the debuff fades after roughly one minute; exact stack interval, duration, and damage are unresolved.
- Local NPC 46304 (`NPC_UNSTABLE_TWILIGHT`) moves to a random point within 5 yards every second. When hit by `SPELL_UNSTABLE_TWILIGHT_DUMMY` (86301), it removes its auras, casts `SPELL_UNSTABLE_TWILIGHT_DAMAGE` (86305) area damage, and despawns after 2.5s. Current Wowhead calls these moving orbs and gives 20k normal/40k heroic examples, with Twilight Zone stacking every 2s for 4k/5k and +5%/+10% Shadow taken; historical Icy Veins says normal players generally leave immediately and gives no current coefficients. Exact aura/radius/mode values are unresolved.
- Local Collapsing Twilight Portal NPC 46301 inherits the Valiona dummy phase, casts 86291, becomes selectable by spell click, and is tracked in an instance GUID set. The periodic summon uses a random Valiona dummy while the repository portal count is at most three (`<= 2` gate). Current Wowhead says a portal appears at a random arena position for one minute before relocating; historical Icy Veins confirms click-to-exit but not cadence. Portal lifetime, count, and realm routing are not fixed.
- Heroic local engage applies Twilight Zone (86210) and the Sentry periodic dummy (93029) through the Valiona aura dummy. NPC 50008 (`NPC_TWILIGHT_SENTRY`) casts Twilight Rift (93017) on summon and owns its summons; NPC 50014 (`NPC_TWILIGHT_RIFT`) inherits the encounter phase. Current Wowhead reports a Sentry roughly every 20s and Rift Blast about 25k Shadow every 3s while alive; local C++ does not expose those periodic timers or the exact Rift Blast identity. Heroic Sentry cadence, target rule, damage, and cleanup remain unresolved.

## Reset, completion, and credit

- `boss_theralion::Reset()` and `boss_valiona::Reset()` call `BossAI::Reset()`, return their event maps to `PHASE_INTRO`, and restore the share-health aura. The helper fields `_dazzlingDestructionCount`, `_deepBreathCount`, `_currentRoomSide`, and `_currentDeepBreathWaypoint` are not all explicitly reinitialized in `Reset()`; liftoff resets only the active phase counter. Whether the engine reconstructs the AIs before a repull is unresolved.
- Evade on either boss calls `_EnterEvadeMode()`, disengages the encounter frame, re-enables gravity, despawns that boss's summons, and calls `_DespawnAtEvade()`. The instance `DATA_THERALION_AND_VALIONA` FAIL path despawns Valiona dummy and Unstable Twilight GUIDs after 30s, clears dummy/collapsing-portal GUID sets, but does not prove retail reset/lockout semantics.
- On death in the ground phase, each AI speaks the death line and casts an instakill spell on the sibling (`95741` from Theralion or `95742` from Valiona); then `_JustDied()` and encounter-frame disengage provide the generic boss-state path. The instance DONE path despawns Valiona dummies and Unstable Twilight, and asks Cho'gall to speak after 6s. Loot, achievement, and retail credit persistence are not asserted from this repository audit.
- `bastion_of_twilight.h` maps boss entries 45993 (Theralion) and 45992 (Valiona) to `DATA_THERALION`/`DATA_VALIONA`, shares encounter state `DATA_THERALION_AND_VALIONA = 1`, and defines supporting entries 46364, 46588, 46374, 46448, 46301, 46147, 46304, 50008, and 50014. The instance is map 671, with entrance/exit door entries 205224/205225. The loader declares and invokes `AddSC_boss_theralion_and_valiona`.
- The TDB 4.3.4 snapshot has base creature rows for 45992/45993 with ScriptName `boss_valiona`/`boss_theralion`, level 88, and base health fields 1,250,000; this is not a four-mode 4.4.2 health table. The current 2023 creature-model update binds model IDs 34812/34813 (WDBVerified 15595). Historical map-671 SQL includes encounter support spawns (including Valiona dummy and Theralion flight target) and difficulty 6, but does not establish the live Classic hotfix state. A 2020 SQL update sets BaseVariance=1 and DamageModifier=120 for both bosses and their variants; this is local history only.

## Material conflicts

- Health: current Wowhead's 4.4.2-labelled 32.2M/54.1M/97.9M/164.9M versus historical Icy Veins 25.8M/48.1M/78.3M/164M; local TDB base health is not mode tuning.
- Phase schedule: current Wowhead 105s Valiona/140s Theralion with Dazzling around 85s and Deep Breath around 100s; historical Icy Veins 100s/140s with transitions around 85s/95s; local movement/event schedule differs and includes unmeasured travel time.
- Timers: Blackout current 45s versus historical 40s versus local 46s; Devouring current 25s/guide twice-per-phase versus local 25s then 41s; Twilight Blast current 3s versus historical 2s versus local 2.4s; Engulfing current 35s versus local 40s; Fabulous current 15–17s versus local 15.7s; Meteorite current 5–6s versus local 1.2s; transition and deep-breath timestamps also disagree.
- Targeting: current Engulfing says 1–3 ranged, historical says one 10-player/two 25-player, local caps one/three and excludes specific classes/meteorite targets; current Meteorite says random while local excludes the Theralion victim and Twilight units; Blackout/Devouring and lane selection are random in local code but exact retail filters are not frozen.
- Damage/radii: current pages disagree between narrative and ability-table examples for Blackout, Devouring Flames, Twilight Blast, Dazzling Destruction, Twilight Meteorite, Fabulous Flames, Twilight Zone, and orb damage. The local C++ confirms splitting/distance/proc relationships but not DBC magnitudes.
- Heroic: current guide reports Twilight Shift five-stack handling and Sentries around 20s, while local spell/periodic setup lacks timing and target details. Historical strategy claims differ on raid composition and target counts.
- Reset/credit: local FAIL/DONE/instakill paths are clear, but helper-field reconstruction, portal/fiend persistence, retail lockout, loot, and achievement credit are not proven.
